## General Questions

**What is eSIM?**
eSIM (Embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card built into your device. Unlike a physical SIM, it is activated electronically — no card insertion needed.

**Does my device support eSIM?**
Most modern smartphones support eSIM. To check:
- iPhone: Settings → General → About → look for EID
- Android: Settings → About Phone → look for EID
- Or dial `*#06#` — if EID appears, your device supports eSIM

**How is El-Sim different from a regular SIM?**
El-Sim is fully digital — no waiting for delivery, no physical card. Purchase, activate, and connect within minutes directly from your profile.

## Usage

**Can I use eSIM and a physical SIM at the same time?**
Yes, if your device supports dual SIM. You can use El-Sim eSIM for data while keeping your physical SIM for calls.

**What happens when my data runs out?**
Your data connection will stop. You can purchase a new plan from your profile at any time.

**Can I renew my plan?**
Yes, you can purchase a new plan at any time from the plans page.

## Technical

**My eSIM stopped working.**
Try the following:
- Restart your device
- Make sure El-Sim eSIM is set as the active data line in your settings
- Check that your plan has not expired
- If the issue continues, contact support

**How do I remove eSIM from my device?**
- iPhone: Settings → Mobile Data → select El-Sim eSIM → Delete eSIM
- Android: Settings → Connections → SIM Manager → select El-Sim eSIM → Delete

**Can I transfer eSIM to another device?**
No. Each eSIM activation code is tied to one device and can only be used once. To use El-Sim on a new device, purchase a new plan.
